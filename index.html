01 / A little introduction

Hello, I'm Fumiya.

A civil engineer interested in how people, technology, and good decisions come together to build better infrastructure.

I'm pursuing an MS in Sustainable Design & Construction at Stanford University. My professional background at Shimizu Corporation spans tunneling and infrastructure projects in Japan and Southeast Asia.

On site, I've worked on construction coordination, planning, and the challenges of delivering projects across cultures. At Stanford, I'm connecting that experience with sustainable design, organizational thinking, and applications of AI.
